# R Projects – UFMG

Academic projects developed in R for the Planning and Analysis of Experiments course at the Federal University of Minas Gerais (UFMG), as part of my MSc in Computational Intelligence.

## Projects

### Case Study 01 – Comparing Students' Body Mass Index

Statistical comparison of the mean Body Mass Index (BMI) of graduate students across two academic semesters.

**Methods:** Exploratory data analysis, data harmonization, two-sample t-tests, assumption checking, confidence intervals, and statistical power analysis.

### Case Study 02 – Comparing Stock Returns

Statistical analysis of the monthly returns of five stocks to investigate differences in their mean returns.

**Methods:** Exploratory data analysis, one-way ANOVA, Tukey's multiple comparisons, hypothesis testing, assumption checking, and statistical power analysis.

## Technologies

- R and R Markdown
- Statistical hypothesis testing
- Data cleaning and visualization
- Statistical modeling and experimental analysis
